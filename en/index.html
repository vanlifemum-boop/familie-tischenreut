Why this site exists

When the youth welfare office took my child, I was alone and lacked information. I want to spare other parents that experience. Here I share my story, explain the legal basics and publicly campaign for the protection of families.

It was a *happy,* *colourful* *childhood.* Trips to the mountains and the sea, birthday parties, Christmas, friends and encouragement. A child who *laughed,* learned and was *loved.* Every day. Then, in a single day, it all ended. *For* *no* *reason.* Without a court order. A room in a *children’s* *home* in place of his own home. But a mother does not stop fighting. *Never.*

— My story. Our story.
