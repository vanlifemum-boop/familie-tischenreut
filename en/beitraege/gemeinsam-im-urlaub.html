Moments together

On holiday together. The world was ours

Paris, Venice and the sea. We discovered the world together.

Some people say you cannot travel with children. We lived the opposite. You stood in front of the Louvre and wanted to know if there was treasure inside. In Venice you chased pigeons and counted gondolas. On every beach you greeted the sea like an old friend.

Travelling with you meant seeing the world twice, once through my eyes and once through yours. Your questions, your wonder and your “Mum, look!” were what we really went for. A big adventure or a short trip to the lake, what mattered was being together.

Nobody can take these memories from us. No order, no report, no case file. They belong to you and me. They are a promise that there is more to come. The world still has plenty in store for both of us. Our list is long, and when you are home again, we will pick up exactly where we were interrupted.

Has this affected you too? Then read the appeal “Let’s make ourselves heard” and get in touch through the contact page. Together we show that these are not isolated cases.
