Appeal

Let’s make ourselves heard

An appeal to affected families in the district of Tirschenreuth and to all parents in Germany whose child has been taken from them.

When your child is taken from you, you fall silent. You have plenty to say, but shame, fear and exhaustion silence you. You are made to feel that you cannot speak. You fear that anything you say will be used against you.

I know that silence. I have lived it myself.

Silence protects those who made mistakes and leaves us unprotected. That is why I am speaking out:

To the families affected in the district of Tirschenreuth

You are not alone. I know there are families in our region who have been through something similar to what happened to my son and me. Let’s make ourselves heard and tell our stories. Speak together, stick to the facts and make ourselves heard. Let’s avoid turning on one another or acting out of anger alone. The more of us speak, the harder it will be to dismiss us as “isolated cases”.

Get in touch with me through the contact page. Every story matters.

To all affected parents in Germany

You are welcome to join me in making ourselves heard. This issue extends beyond our district. Cases of children taken into protective care without sufficient grounds, ignored court orders and flawed proceedings occur across Germany. I document such cases on this site. Your voices turn this into a movement.

The podcast: your stories, at your pace, while protecting your privacy

I want to talk to you and tell your stories in a podcast. And I promise you:

  • You can choose to keep your face off camera. That is absolutely fine. I understand better than I wish I had to.
  • We keep names private. Everyone involved remains anonymous for reasons of privacy. You decide what can be shared.
  • I will also remain anonymous until my court case is over. I do show my face, along with my documents, the correspondence and how the authorities handled my case. In the end, evidence is what matters.

If you want to tell your story on camera, by voice only or anonymously, write to me through the contact page. We will talk through the details privately and at your pace. Nothing will be published without your express consent.

Support the fight for my son

My ten-year-old son was removed from my care without a court order and now lives in a children’s home. I am fighting for his return in court with a lawyer, evidence and everything I have. The legal fees and court costs are more than I can cover alone.

If you would like to help: Join my fundraiser, share it and tell others about it. Every contribution, however small, helps. Every share makes us louder.

Knowing your rights helps protect you

The more you know about your rights, the harder it is to overlook you. Vetopedia is a useful source on taking children into protective care:

  • Vetopedia: resources on taking children into protective care
  • And here on this site: all articles on cases, deadlines and concrete steps.

Why this matters

One family can be called an “isolated case”. Ten families show a pattern. A hundred families show a systemic failure that can no longer be talked away.

Let’s make ourselves heard. For our children.
