Moments together

Cooking together. The kitchen is too quiet

Flour on your nose and dough on your hands. The kitchen was our favourite place.

For some families the living room is the heart of the home. For us it was the kitchen. You sat on your chair with your sleeves rolled up, always in the middle of things. We made our own pasta and you were the strictest quality inspector at the pasta machine. When we cut out biscuits, half the dough mysteriously “disappeared” and your cheeks were suspiciously full.

Cooking was our time together. You told me what had happened at school. We laughed, spilled things and tasted what we were making. You learned to chop, stir and season, and I learned that a meal tastes twice as good with help from little hands.

Today I cook for one. It is quicker, nothing spills and none of the dough disappears. That is the saddest part. A tidy, quiet kitchen is no improvement when a child once laughed there.

Your chair is still there. The pasta machine is waiting. When you are back home, the first thing we will make is your favourite pasta, and you can nibble as much as you like again.

Has this affected you too? Then read the appeal “Let’s make ourselves heard” and get in touch through the contact page. Together we show that these are not isolated cases.
