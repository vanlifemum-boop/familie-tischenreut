Moments together

Christmas without my child

When a place under the tree is left empty.

Christmas was always our celebration. For weeks we made decorations, baked and hung up lights. Every morning you asked how many sleeps were left. On Christmas Eve you stood by the tree in your smart shirt, your eyes shining brighter than any candle.

This year I decorated the tree anyway. I had promised you Christmas would wait for you, even though I did not feel like celebrating. Your presents are wrapped. Your place at the table is set. Everything is here. Except you.

Nobody prepares you for how loud silence can be. For a Christmas when you hear your child’s laughter only in your memories. Among all the families celebrating together sits a mother whose child is sleeping in a children’s home. Nothing had happened to him. An authority made the decision without any judge ever having issued a ruling.

I am telling this story because somewhere another family is getting through its first Christmas without their child and thinks they are alone. You are not alone. There are many of us, and we are getting louder. I am not asking for sympathy.

My boy, your presents are waiting. So am I, for as long as it takes.

Has this affected you too? Then read the appeal “Let’s make ourselves heard” and get in touch through the contact page. Together we show that these are not isolated cases.
